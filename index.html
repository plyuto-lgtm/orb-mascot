States

Static poses rendered with the same geometry as the live one, so you can check the eyes against the sphere. Turn on Show sphere to see the meridians they ride on. Numbers are yaw / pitch in degrees; extra values are eye scale and vertical squash.

Simple bodies

Single outlines. Each carries its own inner sphere for the eyes, so the gaze motion is identical across silhouettes. Pick one in the panel to apply it everywhere.

Composed bodies

Unions of circles, joins filleted by the Rounding slider.

The extra pieces sit on the same sphere as the eyes and turn with the head. The head follows the gaze at a fraction of the angle through its own spring, so looking up rolls the top of the head back and brings the chin forward, and looking sideways swings the near ear inward and the far ear out and back. Each body has its own spring: the bear is snappy, the ghost lazy, the drop under-damped so its sprout overshoots. On top of that, the ghost tail waves and gets a little wilder on fast turns, the cloud's crossed pills tilt with the head, the stack's lower ellipse sways, and the flower's petals breathe in a ripple. A poke pops the eyes and gives the body a small, eased nudge: ears settle back, the lemon stretches a hair, the cloud puffs slightly. An occasional bear ear twitch is available with twitch: true, off by default.

Your own: one piece per line, a circle as x y r or an ellipse as x y rx ry angle, in fractions of the radius, centre at 0 0, y down. Joins are filleted by the Rounding slider.

Colours

Shading is derived from the base colour: a lighter tint towards the top-left, a darker edge. Eyes go dark automatically on light bodies; pass eyeColor to override.

Drop it into a site

<svg id="bot" width="120" height="120"></svg>
<script src="mascot.js"></script>
<script>
  const bot = new Mascot(document.getElementById('bot'), {
    body: 'bear', color: '#1E6DF6', round: 0.5, eye: 'round', eyeLon: 16, eyeLat: 4
  });
  bot.start();            // smoothing, idle, blink, cursor follow

  bot.look(30, -10);      // aim eyes: yaw, pitch (degrees)
  bot.blink(2);           // double blink
  bot.set({ eyeScale: 1.2, squash: 0.5 });  // surprised / happy
  bot.manual = true;      // stop auto gaze while you drive it
</script>

    

    
The right block is the current slider configuration; paste it into the constructor. The mascot is one <svg> with a clip path, so it scales to any size and stays crisp.
